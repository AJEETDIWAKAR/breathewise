# BreatheWise – Competition Demo Script (3–5 Minutes)

**Target Duration:** ~4 minutes (`0:00 – 4:10`)  
**Recommended Setup Before Recording:**
- Browser open to BreatheWise at `1440×900` (clean profile or default `Sharma Household`, live **AirLive** + **Gemini** enabled).
- Keep one mobile emulation tab (`390×844`) ready at the end or resize smoothly for responsive proof.

---

## Walkthrough Timing & Narration

### 1. Dashboard Overview (`0:00 – 0:30`)
- **Action:** Show the **Overview** workspace (`Delhi, India` or active Indian metro). Highlight the sidebar, clean header, live US EPA AQI card, freshness badge (`Fresh` / `AirLive`), Family Safety Summary, and 24-hour trajectory chart.
- **Talking Point:** *"Welcome to BreatheWise — Breathe better, live smarter. Urban residents don't just need a raw AQI number; they need personalized, trustworthy guidance based on their household, health sensitivities, and budget."*

### 2. Location Selection & Privacy Controls (`0:30 – 0:55`)
- **Action:** Click the top header **Location Selector**, type `"Mumbai"` or `"Anand Vihar"`, and select it. Briefly point out the optional **"Use device location"** button and privacy notice.
- **Talking Point:** *"Switching across 280+ Indian cities and Delhi-NCR localities takes a second—or users can optionally click 'Use device location' or enter coordinates manually. Geolocation is strictly opt-in."*

### 3. Real Air Quality Data & Credible Attribution (`0:55 – 1:25`)
- **Action:** Navigate to **Air Quality**, point out **US EPA AQI (0–500)**, **PM2.5 µg/m³**, **Ground Stations count vs. Model Baseline**, clickable **Data by AirLive** attribution, and **Missing Pollutants** notice (`NO2, SO2, O3, CO` marked *Not reported*, never zeroed), plus the India City Ranking table and **"Show data table"** toggle on the chart.
- **Talking Point:** *"Every reading shows its exact scale—US EPA AQI—station aggregation count, timestamp, and clickable AirLive attribution. We never disguise modeled hourly curves as live sensors, and unreported gases are honestly shown as unavailable rather than replaced with zero."*

### 4. Family Profile Setup (`1:25 – 1:55`)
- **Action:** Navigate to **My Family**. Adjust household settings (e.g., `2 adults, 1 child, 1 senior`, toggle **Asthma / respiratory sensitivity**, set budget to **`Low (<= ₹500/mo)`**, commute `Petrol Car`, `18 km`), and click **Save Household Profile**.
- **Talking Point:** *"Next, we tailor the app to a real household: a child, an older adult with asthma sensitivity, an 18 km daily commute, and a strict ₹500 monthly budget. Everything saves locally on-device and can be wiped anytime in Settings."*

### 5. Asking Gemini a Real Question (`1:55 – 2:35`)
- **Action:** Navigate to **AI Assistant**, click the suggested question **"What can I do with a ₹500 monthly budget?"** (or ask `"Should I send my child to play outside?"`), and show the **Gemini (`gemini-4-hydrogen`)** grounded response card.
- **Talking Point:** *"In the AI Assistant, Gemini only receives minimized household traits alongside live AirLive telemetry from our Express server. Notice how the answer cites exact live AQI and PM2.5, admits any missing gases, and separates recommendations by Personal Exposure, Indoor Cleanup, and Personal Emissions."*

### 6. Personalized Daily Routine (`2:35 – 3:00`)
- **Action:** Navigate to **Daily Planner**. Scroll through **Early Morning**, **Midday Window**, and **Evening** blocks highlighting Suggested Action, Recommended Time, Reason, Expected Benefit, Data Behind It, and Limitations.
- **Talking Point:** *"The Smart Daily Planner combines live AirLive levels with Open-Meteo's hourly diurnal curve—never claiming a window is cleaner without data evidence, and always showing limitations."*

### 7. Indoor Air Quality Recommendations (`3:00 – 3:25`)
- **Action:** Navigate to **Indoor Air**. Update room area (e.g., `180 sq ft`), point out the computed **5 ACH CADR (`m³/hr`)**, cooking fuel guidance (`LPG` / `Induction` / `Solid Fuel`), and budget-appropriate options (wet-mopping, cross-ventilation window timing, DIY MERV-13 fan filter).
- **Talking Point:** *"Indoors, BreatheWise calculates exact 5 ACH purifier airflow for the room size while prioritizing zero-cost and sub-₹500 ventilation, cooking exhaust, and dust habits for budget-conscious homes."*

### 8. Logging a Sustainable Action (`3:25 – 3:50`)
- **Action:** Navigate to **Sustainable Choices**, review the Metro/Bus or Home Energy card, then open **Environmental Impact**. Log a `18 km` shift from **Petrol Car** to **Metro Rail** (and optionally log an exposure action like sealing window gaps).
- **Talking Point:** *"When users adopt lower-emission habits, they can log shifts across three distinct pillars—so wearing an N95 mask protects health without falsely claiming carbon savings."*

### 9. Transparent Environmental Impact Calculations & Close (`3:50 – 4:10`)
- **Action:** Click **View calculation methodology** in **Environmental Impact**, show the explicit India GHG Program formula (`(140 - 35 g CO2e/pkm) × 18 km = 1.89 kg CO2e` and `1.29 L` fuel displaced), and conclude on **Overview**.
- **Talking Point:** *"Every kilogram of CO₂e and litre of fuel shows the exact factor used, and we explicitly remind users that personal transport savings do not immediately change city-wide AQI. That's BreatheWise: honest air-quality intelligence for healthier urban living."*
